About PYQtest

Welcome to PYQtest, your dedicated platform for practicing Previous Year Questions (PYQs). We designed this site to provide students with a seamless and distraction-free environment to prepare for their competitive exams.

Our platform allows you to browse through different exam years and shifts, track your attempted and unattempted tests, and easily filter through sessions to optimize your study plan.

With an intuitive interface and a structured approach to problem-solving, PYQtest aims to be your most reliable companion in your exam preparation journey. Good luck!
